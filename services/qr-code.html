Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my QR code?

Basic and Standard orders are delivered within 24 hours. Premium orders with a mini website take 3–5 business days depending on complexity.

What format will I receive my files in?

You will receive a high-resolution PNG file (perfect for digital use and most printing). Standard and Premium orders also include an SVG file which is ideal for large-format printing like banners and billboards.

Can I change what the QR code links to later?

Standard static QR codes link to a fixed URL — if you change your website link you may need a new QR code. Premium mini website clients can request changes and we will update it for you.

Will the QR code work on all phones?

Yes. All QR codes we create are tested and compatible with all modern smartphones — Android and iPhone — using the default camera app, no extra apps required.

How do I pay?

Payment is made before delivery. We accept EFT bank transfer and other payment options discussed on WhatsApp. Once payment is confirmed we begin immediately.
